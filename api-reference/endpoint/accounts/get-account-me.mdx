## Use Case:
Retrieve the current account profile, credit balance, monthly usage, and whether Preview is currently free for you.

<Note>`preview_tier` is `true` while [`POST /preview/leads/`](/api-reference/endpoint/preview/preview-leads) is free for you — that is, once your total spend in the **current calendar month** reaches \$2,000. It flips back to `false` on the 1st. It is not an access flag: permission to call the Preview endpoints is granted separately.</Note>
