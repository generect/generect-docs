## Use Case:
Retrieve a company profile from cached data by Generect ID, LinkedIn URL, domain, or company name.

## Optional sections
The same flags as [Real-time](/api-reference/endpoint/enrich/enrich-realtime-company) apply here: `posts`, `people_also_viewed` and `people_also_follow` each add +1 credit when the section comes back with data.

## Pricing
This is a billable endpoint — you are charged only when a company is found and returned. If nothing is found, the request is free.

<Tip>Your price decreases automatically as your lifetime spend grows. [Check your current tier and pricing →](https://beta.generect.com/settings/billing)</Tip>
