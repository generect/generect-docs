## Use Case:
Retrieve a full lead profile from cached data by Generect ID, LinkedIn URL, or email (reverse lookup).

## Optional sections
The same activity flags as [Real-time](/api-reference/endpoint/enrich/enrich-realtime-lead) apply here: `posts`, `comments`, `reactions` and `people_also_viewed` each add +1 credit when the section comes back with data, while `get_full_experience` and `inexact_company` are free.

## Pricing
This is a billable endpoint — you are charged only when a lead is found and returned. If nothing is found, the request is free.

<Tip>Your price decreases automatically as your lifetime spend grows. [Check your current tier and pricing →](https://beta.generect.com/settings/billing)</Tip>
