## Use Case:
Retrieve a fresh lead profile, including reverse email lookup, by Generect ID, LinkedIn URL, or email.

## Optional sections
Recent LinkedIn activity and the "People also viewed" list are opt-in — send the flag to get the section back:

| Flag | Adds | Extra cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `posts` | The lead's recent posts | +1 credit |
| `comments` | The lead's recent comments | +1 credit |
| `reactions` | The lead's recent reactions (likes) | +1 credit |
| `people_also_viewed` | Profiles LinkedIn lists under "People also viewed" | +1 credit |
| `get_full_experience` | Past positions on top of the current ones | free |
| `inexact_company` | Fuzzy company match when the exact company cannot be resolved | free |

<Note>An extra credit is only charged for a section that actually comes back with data. Requesting `posts` on a lead who never posted costs nothing.</Note>

## Pricing
This is a billable endpoint — you are charged only when a lead is found and returned. If nothing is found, the request is free.

<Tip>Your price decreases automatically as your lifetime spend grows. [Check your current tier and pricing →](https://beta.generect.com/settings/billing)</Tip>
