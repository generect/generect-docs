## Use Case:
Find cached leads at matching companies.

## Pricing
This is a billable endpoint — you are charged for each result returned. If no results are found, the request is free.

A criteria object without its own `limit_by` returns at most 100 rows, so that is the most it is billed for. `data.results_count` is the whole match; page through the rest with `offset_by`.

<Tip>Your price decreases automatically as your lifetime spend grows. [Check your current tier and pricing →](https://app.generect.com/settings/billing)</Tip>
