## Use Case:
Search fresh company data with full realtime filters.

## Pricing
This is a billable endpoint — you are charged for each result returned. If no results are found, the request is free.

## Companies without a website
By default the search leaves out every company that has no website. Send `"include_companies_without_website": true` to also get companies that have a LinkedIn page but no site, such as many hospitals, clinics and local businesses.

- They have no website and no domain, so there are no corporate emails to find for their people, and lead matching by domain does not work for them. An `exclude_domains` entry cannot remove one; use `exclude_ids`.
- Each one is returned and billed as a result like any other. Many of them have few or no leads on LinkedIn, so expect more companies without leads.
- Companies with zero employees on LinkedIn are still left out, and the blocked-domain list still applies to companies that have a website.
- [The realtime count](/api-reference/endpoint/search/count-realtime-companies) comes from LinkedIn and always included them.
